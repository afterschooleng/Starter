Our mission

Engineering should feel possible for every child.

We create welcoming, low-pressure opportunities for children to explore science, technology, engineering, and math in their own community.

Each event centers on a real challenge. Kids ask questions, sketch ideas, build prototypes, test what works, and try again. The goal is not a perfect project—it is confidence, teamwork, creativity, and the joy of figuring something out.
